# ADR 0003 — ETERNALL as the umbrella; imprints named on a publishing theme

Status: Proposed · Date: 2026-11-22

## Context
The platform needs one name and an identity for each module that is memorable on its own. AMAZEEN already has a name and loyal users.

## Decision
ETERNALL is the house: the umbrella and the lifeline dashboard. Modules are **imprints** with their own names, drawn from the world of books and printing: AMAZEEN, ENVOI, FOLIO, ALMANACK, REFRAIN, PROLOGUE, KINDRED, MARGINALIA. Future names follow the rules in `BRAND.md` §2. Every name always appears with its plain function line.

## Consequences
- Strong, ownable sub-brands. AMAZEEN keeps its identity.
- Unusual names need the "name + function" rule everywhere, otherwise they won't be clear.
- Trademark and domain checks are required per name before launch.

## Alternatives considered
- **A mixed "strongest names" set or a celestial theme:** the owner chose the publishing theme for its coherence with AMAZEEN's magazine and the editorial design.
